About the Studio

Magic City Markers

Founded in Billings in 2012, Magic City Markers set out with one mission: bring quality, artistic tattoos to Montana. More than a decade later, we've grown into one of the most respected studios in the state — with a team of multi-style artists, walk-in hours, and a welcoming environment for first-timers and collectors alike.

Walk-Ins Always Welcome — No appointment? No problem. Come in Tue–Sat, noon to 8.

Fully Accessible — Wheelchair accessible location with private parking on-site.

Areola Reconstruction — Specialized tattooing for mastectomy survivors, done with care.

Piercings & Apparel — Full piercing services plus Sullen Art Collective and Ink Addict gear in-studio.
